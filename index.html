Frequently Asked Questions

Is Borobudur climb-up access included?

Yes. Every Borobudur tour we sell includes climb-up access, Upanat sandals and an official local guide. Climb-up is closed every Monday for maintenance.

Is glowing lava at Merapi guaranteed?

No. Lava sightings depend on weather and volcanic activity, so we cannot guarantee them.

What time is hotel pick-up?

Borobudur sunrise is 03:30, temple day tours 07:00 to 09:00, cave tours 07:00 and Merapi lava tours 19:00.

Do you offer private tours with an English-speaking driver?

Yes. Temple and cave tours are private with an English-speaking driver, fuel, parking, tickets and bottled water included. Merapi lava tours use shared transport.

Are prices different for foreigners and locals?

Yes. Foreigners and Indonesian residents pay different official ticket prices. The booking calculator lets you mix both in one group.

How do I book a tour?

Use the booking calculator on this page and send your request by WhatsApp. We confirm availability and the final price.
