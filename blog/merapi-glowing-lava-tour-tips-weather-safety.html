Merapi Glowing Lava Tour: Tips, Weather & Safety

Published by Join Trip Jogja · November 27, 2026

Mount Merapi is alive — and at night, if the conditions are right, you can see it glow.

The tour leaves Yogyakarta at 07:00 PM and takes you to a viewpoint where the volcano fills the horizon. No climbing, no crowds: just you, the dark, and the mountain. Bring a light jacket — the night air up there is cold.

Three Things to Know First

  • Lava is never guaranteed. It depends on weather and volcanic activity. Anyone who promises it is not being honest.
  • Pick the easy viewpoint. You can watch from a comfortable viewpoint — no 1,700-stair hike needed.
  • Dress warm, travel light. Jacket, closed shoes, and a camera that handles low light.

When the glow appears, it is unforgettable. When it doesn't, the night sky and the volcano itself are still worth the trip.
