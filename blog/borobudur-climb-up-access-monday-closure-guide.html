Is Borobudur Closed on Mondays? Climb-Up Access Explained

Published by Join Trip Jogja · November 28, 2026

Planning a Monday visit to Borobudur? Good news: the temple's climb-up access is open every day, including Mondays — so a Monday sunrise is absolutely possible.

Is Borobudur Closed on Mondays?

No. Since July 2025, Borobudur has been open daily, and you can climb to the upper terraces any day of the week. The Monday closure many travelers hear about belongs to Prambanan: its three main temples are closed on Mondays, even though the wider complex stays open.

What Climb-Up Access Means

Climb-up access takes you to the top terraces for the sunrise view, with an official local guide and Upanat sandals. The Borobudur Sunrise tour picks you up at 03:30 AM, so you arrive before dawn. Prefer a later start? The Borobudur + Prambanan tour offers pick-ups at 07:00, 08:00, or 09:00 AM.

How to Plan Around Monday

  • Monday is your only free day? Do Borobudur at sunrise and save Prambanan for another day.
  • Tuesday to Sunday? Combine both temples in one day — the classic route.
  • Arrive early. Sunrise slots and climb-up access are the most popular times of day.
