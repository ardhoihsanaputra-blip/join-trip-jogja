Jomblang Cave and Pindul Cave Tubing in One Day

Published by Join Trip Jogja · November 27, 2026

Some trips you remember. This one you tell stories about for years.

Your day starts at 07:00 AM. By morning you are lowering into Jomblang Cave on a rope, harness on, heart racing — 60 metres down into a hidden world. At the bottom, a beam of sunlight cuts through the dark: the famous "heavenly light".

Then lunch, and a change of pace: Pindul Cave, where you float down an underground river on a tube, headlamp on, rock formations glowing around you.

Why This Day Works

  • Real adventure, done safely — trained cave guides, helmet and harness included.
  • Private tour — just your group, your pace.
  • Two caves, one day — the descent, the light, and the river.

Bring clothes you don't mind getting muddy, a spare dry set, and a waterproof bag for your phone. We'll handle the rest.
