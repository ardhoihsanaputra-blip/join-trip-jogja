Jomblang Vertical Cave and Pindul Cave Tubing Guide from Yogyakarta

Published by Join Trip Jogja · November 27, 2026

Jomblang Cave and Pindul Cave make one of the most adventurous day trips from Yogyakarta. In a single day you descend into a vertical cave on a rope, walk through an ancient forest at the bottom, and then float down an underground river on a tube. It is a full day out, and it is best done as a private tour with a guide and proper safety gear.

Can You Do Jomblang and Pindul in One Day?

Yes. Both caves are in the Gunungkidul area, roughly 1.5 to 2 hours southeast of central Yogyakarta. A 07:00 AM pick-up gets you to Jomblang in time for the morning descent, and Pindul follows in the afternoon, with lunch included in between. Our Jomblang + Pindul Cave tour is private, so the schedule follows your group, not a shared bus.

Suggested One-Day Timeline

  • 07:00 AM: Hotel pick-up in Yogyakarta with a private English-speaking driver.
  • Morning: Arrive at Jomblang Cave, get fitted with a harness and helmet, and descend into the vertical cave with a cave guide.
  • Midday: Walk through the cave to the famous light beam, then lunch at Jomblang.
  • Afternoon: Transfer to Pindul Cave for river tubing through the underground river.
  • Late afternoon: Return transfer to your hotel in Yogyakarta.

What Happens Inside Jomblang Cave?

Jomblang is a vertical cave, so you go down on a rope system operated by the cave team, wearing a harness and helmet. At the bottom, around 60 metres down, you walk through a short tunnel into an ancient forest inside the cave, where a beam of sunlight — the famous "heavenly light" — shines down through the opening above. It is the highlight of the trip, and the best light is usually around midday, which is why the morning departure matters.

Safety Rules to Know Before You Go

The descent is done one person at a time with trained operators, and you must follow the cave guide's instructions at all times. Wear the harness and helmet provided, keep your hands on the rope as instructed, and do not wander off the marked path inside the cave. The cave floor can be muddy and slippery, especially after rain, so proper footwear matters more than fashion. If you are afraid of heights, tell your guide in advance — they do this every day and will talk you through it.

What to Wear and Bring

  • Clothes you do not mind getting muddy: a t-shirt, shorts or quick-dry trousers, and sturdy sandals or shoes with grip.
  • A spare set of dry clothes: you will get wet at Pindul Cave tubing, so bring a change of clothes and a small towel.
  • Waterproof bag or dry bag: for your phone, wallet and camera.
  • Sunscreen and a hat: for the walk between the caves and the open sections of the trip.
  • Cash for personal expenses: everything in the package is included, but small extras are yours.

Is This Tour Right for You?

This tour suits travellers who are comfortable with a rope descent and getting wet. You do not need climbing experience — the operators handle the ropes — but a basic level of fitness helps, and the cave is not suitable for very young children or anyone with a serious fear of heights or enclosed spaces.
