Borobudur Sunrise and Prambanan in One Day from Yogyakarta

Published November 25, 2026 · By Join Trip Jogja · 6 min read

Yes, you can see both Borobudur at sunrise and Prambanan in a single day from Yogyakarta. It makes for a very early start and a full day, but it is one of the most rewarding ways to see Java's two great temple complexes. This guide gives a realistic schedule, what to expect at each stop and how to avoid common mistakes.

Is it possible to do both in one day?

Yes. Borobudur is roughly 1.5 hours from the city by car, and Prambanan sits on the eastern side of Yogyakarta, so the route forms a loop. Going to Borobudur first for sunrise and Prambanan afterwards works well because you finish the day close to your hotel. With a private driver you can also pause for breakfast and a rest in the middle of the day.

Sample one-day itinerary

Times are approximate and change with traffic, season and ticket slots.

  • 03:30 – Hotel pick-up in Yogyakarta. Sleep in the car on the way.
  • 05:00 – Arrive at the Borobudur area, check in for the sunrise ticket and receive your Upanat sandals.
  • 05:15–07:30 – Climb up with your local guide and watch the sky change over the Menoreh hills. Stay for the guide's explanation of the reliefs.
  • 08:00 – Breakfast break (not included in most packages).
  • 10:30 – Drive east toward Prambanan.
  • 12:00–15:00 – Explore Prambanan: the towering central temples, the smaller shrines and the surrounding grounds.
  • 15:30 – Return to your hotel.

Borobudur at sunrise: what to know

Borobudur is the world's largest Buddhist temple. Sunrise visits use a dedicated early ticket, and climbing the upper levels is controlled with official access. Visitors wear special Upanat sandals to protect the ancient stone, and a local guide (Pamong Carita) accompanies the group.

  • Climb-up is closed every Monday for maintenance, so plan your day accordingly.
  • Dress in layers. Mornings can be cool and the steps can be damp.
  • Bring a small flashlight or use your phone light for the first walk.
  • Cloudy mornings are common. Sunrise views are never guaranteed, but the atmosphere is worth it either way.

Prambanan: what to know

Prambanan is a group of 9th-century Hindu temples, known for its tall, pointed towers. After an early morning at Borobudur, the afternoon visit feels very different: more open space, more shade breaks and plenty of photo spots. A guide is optional here and can add the stories behind the carvings.

  • Wear a hat and bring water. Midday can be hot.
  • Modest clothing is appreciated at temple sites.
  • Keep some energy for walking around the complex.

Tips to make the day easier

  • Book a private driver who speaks English, so you can adjust the pace.
  • Choose a package where tickets, parking, fuel and water are included.
  • Avoid Mondays if you want to climb Borobudur.
  • Carry small cash for snacks, drinks and tips.
  • Check the weather the day before and keep your schedule flexible.
