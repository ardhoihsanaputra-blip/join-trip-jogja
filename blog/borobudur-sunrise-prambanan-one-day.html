Borobudur Sunrise and Prambanan in One Day from Yogyakarta

Published by Join Trip Jogja · Updated for jointripjogja.my.id

Yes, you can visit both Borobudur at sunrise and Prambanan Temple in a single day from Yogyakarta. It starts very early before dawn and covers two major Central Java heritage sites in one well-paced private route.

Is It Possible to Do Both in One Day?

Yes. Borobudur sits roughly 1 to 1.5 hours northwest of central Yogyakarta depending on pick-up location and traffic, while Prambanan lies east of the city toward Klaten. Starting at 03:30 AM lets you catch sunrise at Borobudur, continue toward Prambanan in the late morning or afternoon, and return to your hotel comfortably.

Suggested One-Day Timeline

  • 03:30 AM: Hotel pick-up in Yogyakarta with an English-speaking driver.
  • 04:45–06:30 AM: Arrival, climb-up access with official local guide and Upanat sandals, and sunrise viewing at Borobudur.
  • 07:30–09:30 AM: Breakfast break and overland transfer across Central Java toward Prambanan.
  • 10:30 AM–01:30 PM: Explore Prambanan Temple complex and surrounding sanctuary compounds.
  • Afternoon: Return transfer to your hotel in Yogyakarta.
Practical Planning Notes

Borobudur structure climb-up access is closed every Monday for maintenance. If you want to compare package options or calculate group pricing, visit the tour packages or booking calculator on the main page.
